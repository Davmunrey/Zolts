# 00 — Executive summary

## Bottom line

1. The GTM stack is broken by **fragmentation**: 12-20 tools, business logic trapped in unversioned Clay tables, credits burned without a P&L, attribution that cannot be defended in front of a CFO.
2. Zolts does not compete on data (commodity, capital-intensive, legally toxic to own) or on AI copy (commoditised by LLMs). It competes on the **execution runtime**: the versioned graph that decides *who, when, through which channel, with which message*, and proves the lift with holdouts.
3. "Adaptable to any company" is solved by an **overlay architecture** (Blueprint → Industry Pack → Tenant → Program), not by bespoke services. Services destroy the multiple.
4. The central trade-off is **vertical depth versus horizontal breadth**. Recommendation: horizontal in primitives, vertical in blueprints. Sell vertical, build horizontal.
5. The scarce asset at 24 months is neither the data nor the model: it is **reputable sending capacity** and the **outcome dataset**. Both accrue only through execution, starting on day one.

## Explicit trade-offs

| Decision | Cost | Why it is accepted |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse-native (bring your own data) | Longer sales cycle, requires customer data maturity | Removes the "data hostage" objection, unlocks enterprise, cuts storage COGS |
| Do not own B2B data | Dependence on providers | Multi-provider routing captures margin with no capex and no GDPR ownership liability |
| Mandatory holdout (5-10%) | Reduces reachable volume, adds commercial friction | Makes the CFO an ally; the only defence against the attribution war |
| Agents gated by evals | Less demo magic, slower | A single hallucinated email naming a customer logo destroys the enterprise account |
| Config-as-code over visual UI | More upfront engineering | Without versioning and tests there is no governance; without governance there is no enterprise and no price |

## Recommended decision

Build the **runtime, policy engine and measurement layer** — the defensible core. Buy or partner for **data, sending channels and dialers** — mature commodity supply. Never build: a proprietary contact database, or a CRM.

Entry wedge: **B2B companies of 50-500 employees running a mixed motion (PLG plus sales-led) in Europe**, where compliance pain and fragmentation peak and US incumbents are weakest.
